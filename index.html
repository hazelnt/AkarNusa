What is AkarNusa ?

Akar Nusa is a non-profit organization focusing on the environment and education. Our mission is to raise awareness and encourage the younger generation to take concrete action towards sustainable development. Our work has been recognized by the International Baccalaureate (IB), including through funding support and opportunities that have enabled us to further develop and expand our sustainability and education initiatives Akar Nusa’s programs include Akar NuAction, Akar NuCycle, Akar Nusa Tales and GreenLeap.
